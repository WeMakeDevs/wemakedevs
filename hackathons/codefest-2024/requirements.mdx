## What to build

Release an iOS (iPhone) or Android app to the App Store or Google Play Store that uses the RevenueCat SDK to power at least one in-app purchase. The 1st version of your app **must** be released in the store during **between August 5th and September 18**.

## What to submit

Each submission must contain the following stuff:

- Short **description** of the app (may be the same as the App Store/Play Store description)
- **URL** to your app's page on the App Store and/or Google Play Store
- 1024 x 1024 uncropped image of your **App Icon**
- At least one **screenshot** of the app without device frames.
  - Preferred resolution of 1179px width by 2556px height
- OPTIONAL: We recommend including a video demonstration of your app, but this is not required.
- App must either offer a **free trial** or the submission must include a promo code for judges to unlock the in-app purchases for testing all premium features.
- For apps that want to be eligible for the **#BuildInPublic Award**:
  - Include links to any relevant social accounts and posts/threads where progress was shared in public.
  - Include a description of how building in public helped benefit the app (motivation, feedback from others, etc)
