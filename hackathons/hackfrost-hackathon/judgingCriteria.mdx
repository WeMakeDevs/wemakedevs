# Judging Criteria

- Use of Kestra
  Does the project make use of Kestra
- Potential Impact
  Includes the extent to which the solution can address a need.
- Creativity & Originality
  Includes creativity and originality of the idea.
- Learning
  Did the team members learn something while building the project?
- Implementation of the Idea
  Includes how well the idea was executed by the developer/team.
- Aesthetics
  How is the UI/UX of the project? Is it intuitive and user-friendly?
- Wow Factor
  Does the project have a unique aspect that makes it stand out, even if it lacks in other criteria?
- Presentation
  How good is the video? For every 10 seconds over the time limit the presentation video is, we will dock 1 point from the total score. You get the first additional 10s for free as discretionary time to account for errors and slide transitions etc.
