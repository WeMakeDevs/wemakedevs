01 · The Gap

Students are learning Oracle. They just aren't joining Oracle.

A

Institution-gated. Oracle Academy memberships are for schools. Oracle's own site states that "individual students may not join as members," while competitors recruit students directly.

B

Educator-first, not builder-first. The new Oracle Academy Community serves faculty and ambassadors. There is no peer-led home for student developers.

C

Solo, not social. Race to Certification drew 535,000+ learners in 2025, proof the demand exists. But they certify alone and leave. No community catches them.

Developers don't switch stacks.
They keep the one they started on.
